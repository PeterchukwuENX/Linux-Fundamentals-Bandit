# Linux-Fundamentals-Bandit
Linux Fundamentals — Bandit Walkthrough

I'm working toward a SOC (Security Operations Center) analyst role, and this repo is where I'm documenting my way through OverTheWire's Bandit, a wargame that teaches Linux fundamentals through a series of levels — each one hands you a small puzzle that only makes sense once you understand a specific Linux concept (file permissions, hidden files, process inspection, and so on).

I'm not just solving these and moving on. For every level, I'm writing down:

The concept — what Linux idea the level is actually testing, in plain English
The command(s) — exactly what I ran to solve it
The why — what that command is doing under the hood, and why it works

The goal is to actually understand the "why," not just collect passwords and move to the next level.

Structure

Each level has its own file: level00.md, level01.md, and so on. Open any one of them to see the format.

Progress
Currently working through the early levels. This section will get updated as I go.
Why this matters
A big part of SOC work is being able to read logs, navigate a Linux system under pressure, and explain why something is happening — not just recognize that it is. Bandit is a low-stakes way to build that muscle before it matters in a real environment.
